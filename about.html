Our Mission

At FrameFocus Photography, we aim to freeze moments in time with creativity, authenticity, and precision. Our work is not just about taking pictures – it's about capturing emotions, stories, and timeless memories for every client.

Meet the Owner
Prathamesh Rane

Founder & Lead Photographer at FrameFocus Photography, Prathamesh Rane is a visionary with an eye for detail and a heart that beats for storytelling. With over a decade of experience in professional photography, he has transformed countless moments into timeless masterpieces. His expertise lies in luxury wedding shoots, editorial fashion campaigns, cinematic pre-wedding stories, and destination events across the globe.

A perfectionist at heart, Prathamesh combines technical mastery with emotional depth — ensuring every frame is not just seen, but felt. He has trained under renowned global photographers, and his work has been featured in leading publications like Vogue India, National Geographic Explorer, and Better Photography. Beyond the lens, he is also a mentor, regularly conducting masterclasses to nurture aspiring photographers.

Whether he's capturing the soft smiles of a bride, the chaos of a bustling cityscape, or the serenity of nature — Prathamesh believes every photo should speak volumes, even in silence.
